# Baseline: Verified Pitch Facts

Research date: 2026-10-09. Every number below was checked against the source listed. Where only secondary coverage was available, the confidence note says so. **If a number isn't in this file, don't put it on a slide.**

Confidence key: **High** = read in the primary source (government doc / company filing / official report). **Med** = primary source found, but the exact figure came from reputable secondary coverage or a summary of the primary. **Low** = secondary only / could not verify.

## Top 5 facts for the 3-minute pitch

1. **Americans 60+ reported $7.7 billion in losses to the FBI in 2025, up 59% in one year, with an average loss of $38,500, the highest of any age group.** (FBI IC3 2025 Internet Crime Report, Apr 2026; §1.8)
2. **"AI has fully defeated" voiceprint authentication, OpenAI's Sam Altman told a Federal Reserve conference in July 2025, and a reporter had already shown it by getting into his own Lloyds account with a free ElevenLabs clone.** (AP, 2025-07-22; Vice, Feb 2023; §2.1–2.2)
3. **Pindrop counted deepfake fraud attempts on contact centers rising more than 1,300% in 2024, from about one a month to seven a day, across 1.2 billion calls.** (Pindrop 2025 Voice Intelligence & Security Report, vendor data; §2.3)
4. **Bank impersonators were the costliest business-impersonation scam in 2025, part of a record ~$16 billion Americans reported losing to fraud, and older adults' $100K+ impersonation losses grew eightfold from 2020 to 2024 ($55M to $445M).** (FTC, Jun 2026 and Aug 2025; §1.1–1.3, §4.1)
5. **Since October 2024, UK banks have had to reimburse scam victims up to £85,000 a claim, returning 88% of money lost. In the US, scams where the victim sends the money still fall outside Reg E, so it's the bank that chooses whether to stop the coached customer.** (UK PSR, 2024–2025; CFPB Reg E; §3.1–3.6)

Backup one-liners:
- AWS shut down Amazon Connect Voice ID on 20 May 2026, so the cloud giants are leaving voice authentication just as cloning gets cheap. (§5)
- 4,214 US credit unions serve 146 million members, and almost none can afford an enterprise voice-security contract. (§5)
- The Senior Safe Act already shields trained bank staff who report suspected elder exploitation. Baseline gives them the evidence to do it. (§4.6)

## 3. Liability and regulation

### 3a. US: Regulation E (who eats the loss)

| # | Claim | Number | Source | URL | Date | Confidence |

| 3.1 | Under Reg E (12 CFR 1005.2(m)), an "unauthorized" EFT is one started by someone other than the consumer without actual authority and from which the consumer gets no benefit. **Market sizing hook:** 4,214 federally insured US credit unions with 146.1M members as of 30 Jun 2026, and the count keeps shrinking through consolidation (4,411 a year earlier). NCUA Q2 2026 release: https://ncua.gov/newsroom/press-release/2026/ncua-releases-second-quarter-2026-credit-union-system-performance-data (2026-09). Confidence: High.

### How Baseline differentiates (talk track)
1. **Three independent baselines, fused.** (a) Is it the enrolled customer? (speaker verification); (b) does it sound human? (spoof detection); (c) does the request match a known scam script? (LLM + cited playbook). Most products sell (a)+(b) (Pindrop, Gatekeeper) or only (b) (Reality Defender, Resemble). Google does a consumer-side (c). **No one we found gives a small institution's agent all three, live, with the evidence on screen.** A clone that beats voice-ID still trips (b) and (c), and a genuine but coached senior trips (c) alone, which voice-security products are not built to catch.
2. **Catches the case voice-auth can't: the real customer being coached.** That's the authorized-push-payment loss that the UK already makes banks pay (up to £85K/claim) and US regulators keep pressuring on.
3. **On-prem / one box.** Call audio never leaves the bank. It runs on a single laptop or GPU box, with no per-call cloud fees, which matters as AWS Voice ID exits.
4. **Priced for credit unions.** There are 4,214 of them, serving 146M members, many older and lower-income, and most can't sign a Pindrop-scale enterprise contract.
5. **Honest signals.** No lie detection, no agent emotion scoring (EU-prohibited), and measured accuracy shown on held-out data.
